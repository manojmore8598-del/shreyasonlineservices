Who We Are

A Local Centre for Online & Documentation Services

Shreyas Online Services is a digital documentation and online application assistance centre based in Pen, District Raigad, Maharashtra. We help individuals and businesses navigate common online forms, government certificates, and registration processes with clear, practical guidance.

Whether you need help with a Food License application, passport paperwork, Udyam registration, caste or other certificates, gazette-related services, or everyday printing and scanning — our goal is to make the process easier to understand and complete.

We are located at Gala No. 23, Parmar Plaza, near Pen Police Station, beside Krushna Dairy — a convenient stop for customers from Pen and surrounding areas.

Our Story

Shreyas Online Services was set up to serve the everyday documentation and online application needs of people in Pen and nearby towns. Many customers find government portals and paperwork confusing — we aim to bridge that gap with patient, step-by-step assistance at a local centre they can visit in person.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, easy-to-understand assistance for online applications and documentation so customers can complete their paperwork with greater clarity and less stress — right here in Pen, Raigad.

Our Service Commitment

We focus on honest guidance: explaining what documents are typically needed, helping prepare and submit applications where appropriate, and directing customers to the right next step. We do not claim government authorisation or guaranteed approvals.

Important: Shreyas Online Services provides application assistance and documentation support. Government departments make the final decision on all approvals. We do not offer Aadhaar enrollment, updates, or other Aadhaar-related services.
